# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-14

### Included

- Local keyboard sounds with 11 built-in themes.
- Persistent volume and sound controls.
- Today's keystrokes, total keystrokes, and total clicks.
- Heatmap, trends, application ranking, recent activity, and menu bar popup.
- Configurable muted shortcuts while statistics remain active.
- Silent modifier keys.
- Single-instance behavior, permission health, and compatibility with existing local data.
- Apple Silicon support for macOS 26 and 27.

### Not Included

- Quiet hours and per-application muting.
- Custom sounds.
- WPM displays.
- Achievements.

### Distribution

- Distributed outside the App Store without Apple Developer ID signing or notarization.
- First launch requires explicit approval through macOS Privacy & Security, as documented in the README.
